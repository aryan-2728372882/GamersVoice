Consumer Protection

Refund Policy

We stand behind the engineering of GamerVoice. Here is our straightforward refund policy for all digital VIP purchases.

1. 48-Hour Refund Window

If you purchase a GamerVoice VIP pass (Weekly ₹29, Monthly ₹89, or Lifetime ₹249) and experience technical carrier incompatibility or the application fails to perform as described on your specific Android hardware, you are eligible for a 100% refund within 48 hours of purchase.

2. Eligibility Criteria

Refunds are processed in the following scenarios:

  • Payment was successfully deducted via Razorpay but VIP perks failed to link to your user account.
  • Accidental duplicate transaction charged for the same user account.
  • Critical microphone hardware incompatibility that our engineering team cannot resolve within 24 hours.

3. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, email supportgamersvoice@gmail.com with the subject line "VIP Refund Request" and include:

  • Your registered account email address.
  • Your Razorpay Payment ID (starts with pay_...).
  • A brief explanation of the technical issue encountered.

4. Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are processed through Razorpay directly back to your original source of payment (UPI bank account, Paytm wallet, or credit/debit card) within 5 to 7 business days.
