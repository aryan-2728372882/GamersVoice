Privacy Guarantee

Privacy Policy

GamerVoice was built with a fundamental principle: your voice belongs to you and your squad, not a cloud server.

1. Zero Cloud Voice Recording

Unlike traditional chat services that route your microphone stream through intermediate cloud processing, GamerVoice operates on a pure WebRTC peer-to-peer (P2P) mesh. Your voice packets are encrypted end-to-end using DTLS-SRTP and travel directly between players' phones. We do not record, store, transcribe, or listen to any squad audio.

2. On-Device 120s Clutch Audio Highlights

Our personal clutch highlight feature maintains a temporary 2-minute rolling PCM buffer in your mobile device's volatile RAM. When you trigger the save button, that audio is packaged into a .wav file directly in your private device storage (/Music/GamerVoice_Clips/). Nothing is ever sent to our servers.

3. Information We Collect

To provide account authentication, VIP synchronization, and referral crediting, we store:

  • Your account email address and gamer handle (managed securely through Firebase Authentication).
  • Entitlement metadata (active VIP tier, purchase timestamp, and referral redemption flags).
  • Ephemeral signaling connection data (anonymized IP addresses used solely for ICE candidate negotiation).

4. No Third-Party Advertising SDKs

We do not bundle AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, or any commercial tracking SDKs into the GamerVoice APK. Your mobile device resources and network data are never used for targeted surveillance.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, contact us at supportgamersvoice@gmail.com.
