Privacy Guarantee

Privacy Policy

GamerVoice was built with a fundamental principle: your voice belongs to you and your squad, not a cloud server.

1. Zero Server Voice Recording & Storage

GamerVoice transmits squad voice packets using WebRTC peer-to-peer connections (with encrypted relay fallback when strict Carrier-Grade NAT requires it). Voice streams are encrypted in transit using DTLS-SRTP. Normal squad voice is never uploaded, recorded, transcribed, or stored on GamerVoice servers.

2. On-Device 120s Clutch Highlights (Local RAM Buffer)

Our optional personal clutch highlight feature operates strictly on your local device. When enabled in Settings, it maintains a temporary 120-second circular audio buffer in your device's volatile RAM. This audio is never transmitted over the internet or uploaded to any server. When you explicitly tap "Save Clutch Clip", the buffered audio is exported as a local .wav file directly into your device's private app directory (Android/data/com.gamervoice.app/files/Music/GamerVoice_Clips/). If not saved, the rolling buffer is automatically overwritten in RAM or purged immediately when the session ends.

3. Information We Collect

To provide account authentication, VIP synchronization, and referral crediting, we store:

  • Your account email address and gamer handle (managed securely through Firebase Authentication).
  • Entitlement metadata (active VIP tier, purchase timestamp, and referral redemption flags).
  • Ephemeral signaling connection data (anonymized IP addresses used solely for WebRTC negotiation).

4. No Third-Party Advertising SDKs

We do not bundle AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, or any commercial tracking SDKs into the GamerVoice APK. Your mobile device resources and network data are never used for targeted surveillance.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, contact us at supportgamersvoice@gmail.com.
