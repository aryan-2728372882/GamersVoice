Common Fixes

Instant Troubleshooting Tips

  • Mic turns off when game starts: Disable Battery Optimization for GamerVoice in Android Settings.
  • Floating HUD not visible: Ensure "Display Over Other Apps" permission is allowed.
  • VIP not appearing after purchase: Tap "Refresh Entitlements" in Profile or restart the application.
